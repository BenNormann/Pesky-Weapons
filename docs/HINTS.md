# HINTS: what to tell a crew that is stuck (2026-10-14)

The rooms carry no signs any more (owner's rule; a narrator will hint later). Until the narrator exists this
file is the narrator: when a crew has been in a room too long without progress, give them the next line down.
Three steps per room, each one more direct than the last. Give the first after about two minutes with no
progress, the second two minutes later, the third when they ask for it. Lines are written to be read aloud
or pasted into chat as they are. Weapon masses the plates care about: Hammer 14, Mace 8, Orb 4, Sword 3,
Staff 2.5, Dagger 1, Banana 0.5.

## Any room

- A weapon moves only by looking and launching. Hold Shift to stand up and stay put. Q leaves the weapon;
  a free soul flies, and passes any OPEN door, but can do nothing else.
- A weapon lying still is furniture to a goblin. Movement is what they hunt.
- Everything purple is the goblins' magic. Bright, drifting purple is live; dull purple-grey is dead. Every
  teleport door is fed by a wire; follow the wire and you find what feeds it.
- Nobody is ever stuck for good: a weapon that falls out of the world comes back to the last place it lay
  still; Q and a new weapon from the start room is always an option.

## Room 3: THE GATEHOUSE (the portcullis, the winch chamber and the guards' yard)

1. "Follow the wires. One comes out of the floor at a plate in the yard and runs into the gate passage; the
   gate is up only while something heavy sits on that plate, and whoever holds it cannot also go through."
2. "The other wire into the passage comes down from the winch chamber inside the wall, above the gate, which
   opens onto the FAR side. A lever up there pins the winch with the gate up; after that the plate can be left.
   The stair up the wall's back face takes anyone; a blade can climb the timber post instead."
3. "Mace or Hammer sits still on the hold plate (8 kg; the plate's wire, the junction and the winch drum all
   light). Another weapon passes under the raised gate, hops up the stair on the back of the gate wall into the
   chamber and hits the lever: the gate stays up, the heavy follows. Behind the gate, two guards and a plate
   hidden behind the crates in the far left corner: it wants 10 kg at once (a Hammer, or a Mace with a Sword or
   an Orb on it); its wire lights up the wall into the exit door and the portal comes alive."

## Room 4: WELL ROOM (the hanging platform and the rope)

1. "The lift is stuck up there, hanging on one rope. Rope cuts."
2. "Only a blade sticks in timber, and only a stuck blade can climb it. Throw yourself point-first at one of
   the wooden posts. Stuck, look at the post and launch: the blade hops straight up it and stabs back in
   where it stops rising. The rope runs from the top of the platform over the wheel to the cleat on the
   ledge."
3. "Sword or Dagger: stick in a post, climb it to the ledge by the entry, then throw yourself at the rope
   (anywhere along it, the slant to the cleat is the easiest). The platform falls and becomes a ramp. The
   Mace or the Hammer rides the ramp up to the far balcony and sits on the plate (8 kg). It latches."

## Room 5: PORTER ROOM (the hall, the grate and the porter)

1. "The exit only opens once the hall is clear. The grate opens only from inside."
2. "Bang the bars and someone comes to see. The porter carries small things through the bars to its stand.
   Only a Dagger fits, and only a still one."
3. "Kill the two goblins in the hall. The Dagger lies still right against the bars; any weapon hitting the
   grate rings it, and the porter walks over and pulls the Dagger through. Inside, the Dagger launches into
   the lever on the back of the partition: the grate rises for good. Everyone through to the exit."

## Room 6: CIRCUIT ROOM (the generator and the dead wire)

1. "This is where the labyrinth's power comes from. Four wires leave the generator lit. One leaves dead. The
   dead one is yours."
2. "Follow the dead wire: down into the pit and up to the gallery. It is broken twice. Metal lying still
   across a break closes it, and the wire lights up to the next break."
3. "Two Sword-length metal bodies (Sword, Mace or Hammer; a Dagger is too short) lie still across the two
   breaks, one on the pit floor, one on the gallery, at the same time. The breaks pull a nearby metal body
   into place by themselves. When both are closed the circuit seals itself, the weapons may leave, and the
   exit door's dead portal comes alive."

## Room 7: THE CHASM (the fallen bridge)

1. "The bridge was magic, and its wire is broken on THEIR side. Nobody can jump that far."
2. "A braced friend can be sent much farther than any launch. Hold Shift to stand up. A heavy launching into
   a braced friend bats it along the heavy's aim."
3. "The Dagger stands braced on the lip (Shift). The Hammer or the Mace launches straight into it, aimed at
   the far bank. The Dagger lands among two goblins and lies still until they lose interest, then creeps to
   the short break beside the bridge's end and lies across it: the bridge appears. Everybody crosses to the
   exit. Fell in? Metal is lifted out by the magnet on the near lip; the Staff and the Banana climb the stair
   along the near bank's face."

## The start room and the gate hall

- Start: "Fly into a weapon on a rack to possess it. Every rack has one of each kind; choose by the job
  ahead: blades stick in wood, heavies hold plates and bat friends, the Dagger fits where nothing else does."
- Gate hall (the labyrinth's exit): "The way out is the one lit doorway. Everyone still standing has to be
  within the ring in front of it at the same moment; the round ends when the last one arrives."
