Pesky Weapons: rooms v3, floor plans

Companion to rooms-v3.md (2026-10-02 owner notes applied). Plans are to scale, 10 drawing units per metre, dimensions in metres. Long rooms (1, 3, 5) are drawn with NORTH TO THE LEFT and EAST UP; the Well Room and the Circuit Room have north at the top. Numbered callouts match the beats in the text. "y" is height above the room's floor. Every mandatory jump is annotated with the weakest weapon that must make it and its margin from the ballistics (g 20; Dagger 14, Banana 13, Sword 12, Staff 11, Mace 10, Hammer 9 m/s).

Legend

Room 1: ARMORY GATE

Long gallery 56 x 18 m, 9 m high. Beats: 1 HOLD (Mace parks on P1, portcullis G1 lifts while held), 2 PIN (Sword hits L1 beyond the gate, G1 latches), 3 WEIGH IN (both bodies on P2 in the trench, 11 kg against 10). North is left.

Room 2: WELL ROOM

Octagon 40 m flat to flat, 14 m high: an elevator shaft. A 26 x 26 m platform hangs level at +9 m on a rope (north edge) and a chain (south edge) over two wheels. Beats: 1 CLIMB (a blade zigzags up the north guide frame to the north balcony), 2 CUT (the rope at its cleat; the platform swings down about its chained south edge into a 20.3 deg ramp), 3 RAMP AND WEIGH (the heavy hops up the ramp onto the south balcony and sits on P, 8 kg, by the exit). North is up.

Room 3: PORTER ROOM

Hall 40 x 16 m ending in a 9.5 m partition (1.5 m slot under an 11 m roof), control room 12 x 10 m beyond. Beats: 1 SNEAK AND READ (past the patrol; the chains' ends are in the hall), 2 LURE AND RIDE (a landing near the control door brings the porter out; the light body lies still and is carried in; the heavy is too heavy to lift), 3 LEVER (the rider hits L_DOOR, told which by the reader; L_PEN frees the pen into the hall), 4 FIGHT (the heavy comes in and kills the two shield guards; the exit opens when the control room is cleared). North is left.

Room 4: CIRCUIT ROOM

Rectangle 24 x 30 m, 8 m high, no enemies, no pits. A source, a glowing wire with two gaps, a 2.5 m ledge. Beats: 1 LOW GAP (a metal body lies across gap 1 on the floor), 2 HIGH GAP (a blade hops onto the ledge and lies across gap 2; both closed together for 1 s latches the exit). The Staff and the Banana do nothing on a gap. North is up.

Room 5: BAT ROOM (finale, holds the EXIT)

Rectangle 24 x 52 m, 24 m high. Entry at floor level, steps up to a 6 m dais, a 14 m chasm, the south bank at 6 m with the exit. Beats: 1 STEPS, 2 BAT (the heavy bats the braced light across, 20 m/s), 3 LEVER (the light hits L_BRIDGE; the drawbridge swings down, the exit opens), 4 CROSS. North is left.
