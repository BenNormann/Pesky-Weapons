Back tower, rooms 6 to 20 (+ a, b, c, d): the hard redesign

Paper design. Path unchanged: 5 → 6 → 7 → 8 → 9 (rest, Staff; b off it, key 2) → 10 → 11 → 12 → 13 → 14 → stairs → 15 → 16 (d off it) → 17 → 18 (c off it) → 19 (winch) → 20 (boss) → bridge. a is the lift bottom beside room 2.

1. The rules this design follows

  1. Big rooms, several puzzles. Every tower room is huge and tall; it holds 2 to 4 lettered stations built from kit we already have, spun a new way.
  2. Hard after the tutorial. From room 6: no answer signs, multi-step plans, holds, baits and timing between players.
  3. Bring your own bodies. Rooms supply no weapons. Rack in 1 (Sword, Dagger, Mace), Hammer in 4, Staff in 9. A wrong loadout costs a trip back (or the lift once it runs).
  4. Possessed-weapon-only. Each puzzle carries a LITMUS line: "a human carrying this weapon could not, because ...".
  5. Team first, solo possible. Built for 2-4 acting at once; 1 player parks bodies and makes more trips. Seats, timers and enemies scale with player count.
  6. No soft-locks, network-sane. Everything latches or resets; falls land somewhere you can climb from; a lost body is recovered by soul or by breaking it home. State = a few host values; movers follow the shared clock; no shared physics props.

Possessed-weapon verbs used. V1 Second launch (get into a space no hand fits, then act again in there). V2 Soul through the bars (the mind goes where no body can, and wakes a body that came another way). V3 Dead object (goblins ignore, tidy, carry, even wield a still weapon). V4 Self-removing fixture (a body is the rung, keystone, wedge or rod, then frees itself and follows). V5 One mind, two places (make the noise here, be the clutter over there a second later). V6 Freight and ammunition (magnet hoists, chutes, lifts and drops that only take a weapon). V7 Death on purpose (break to go home; the lift makes it a route).

Sketch key: yellow letter = puzzle station · purple bar = doorway · brown = wood (blades stick) · purple dashed = SoulGrate (souls pass, nothing else) · blue dashed = clock mover / lift · green dot = goblin · red = magnet · grey = pit or lower level (always has a way back up).

2. Rooms

6 · Rope Room tall shaft · hard

Puzzles A Rope loft. The rope holding the drop-bridge runs inside a sealed loft. Its only opening is an arrow slit a Dagger fits; the rope is round a corner inside. B Rung wall. The bridge level is too high for the heavy bodies; the only way up is a plank wall. C Dead weight. Past the fallen bridge a heavy plate opens the high exit.

Team P1 (Dagger) flies through the slit, lands, turns the corner, launches again and cuts the rope; leaves by the slit. Meanwhile P2 (Sword) sticks into the plank wall as rung 1; P1 joins as rung 2. P3 (Mace) hops rung, rung, ledge. A curious goblin walks the floor: rungs hold still while it looks, and a teammate lures its gaze away before each hop (a goblin poke knocks a rung out). Mace crosses the bridge and rests on the plate. Rungs pull out and follow.

Solo Dagger cuts the rope. Stick Sword as rung 1, soul to Dagger, stick as rung 2, soul to Mace, climb, plate. Soul back for the blades, one at a time.

Loadout Dagger + Sword + Mace (or Hammer). No Dagger = trip back to the rack.

Litmus A: a thrown dagger cannot land in a loft no arm fits, turn a corner and launch itself a second time. B: the ladder is made of minds: each rung frees itself and follows; alone, one mind is rung, climber and rung again. C: only completes A+B (the weight is a body that climbed a ladder of teammates).

Scaling 1: no goblin, 2 rungs. 2: one curious goblin. 3: two goblins, 3 rungs (wider gaps). 4: two goblins, 3 rungs, plate wants two heavy bodies or Hammer.

Reset Rope and plate latch. A fall lands on the shaft floor. A Dagger stuck in the loft can always launch out by the slit, or Q and walk away as a soul through the loft's grate.

Kit Rope + DropRamp (Drop mode), WoodSurface, Plate, Door_Plate, curious goblin. NEW ShapedOpening (slit), SoulGrate.

7 · Barred Door wedge · connector, hard

Puzzles The tower door stays barred (it opens later from the yard). A doorkeeper goblin walks a loop; the keeper gate to the way up opens only while he stands at it, and he faces the room the whole time. A Gong niche out of his sight. B The doormat, in plain view at his feet. C Wedge lever beyond the gate latches it open.

Team P2-P4 lie still on the doormat as clutter. When the keeper reaches the gate, P1 strikes the gong in the niche and freezes: the keeper turns to stare at the noise with the gate open behind him. The doormat bodies launch through behind his back. One hits C; the gate latches; P1 walks through at leisure.

Solo Park body B on the doormat. With body A hit the gong, Q at once, fly to B (goblins ignore souls), possess, launch through, hit C. Come back for A.

Loadout Anything, but two bodies minimum solo.

Litmus: a human cannot lie at a guard's feet as harmless clutter, and cannot ring a gong at one end of a hall and be inside a different body at the other end a heartbeat later.

Scaling 1: keeper only, long stare. 2: + a lookout whose cone sweeps the doormat on the clock (go between sweeps). 3-4: two lookouts on opposite sweeps, shorter stare; seen moving = both come and the gate shuts (keeper leaves his post). Lie still 5 s and try again.

Reset Gate never latches until C; hostile goblins calm down; nothing is consumed.

8 · Pot Room hexagon · hard

Puzzles The exit lever is in one of five big pots inside a pen fenced by a full-height SoulGrate: no weapon gets in, goblins use their own gap. A potter goblin with empty hands lives inside. A The sill: a hatch where a still weapon can be left within his reach. B Scouting: pots have grated lids; two hold alarm bells that wake the pen's sleepers. C The bait line round the fence.

Team Souls dip into the pots and call which one holds the lever and which hold bells. P1 (Mace) lies still on the sill; the potter picks it up as his club. P2 shows himself moving outside the fence directly behind the lever pot: the potter charges and swings at him through the pot, and the Mace's blow smashes it. P3 repeats behind the lever itself (any impact flips it). The lever opens exit and pen; P1 launches out of his fist and leaves by the pen gate.

Solo Scout as a soul. Leave the Mace on the sill (it needs no mind in it to be swung). Bait as the Sword behind the right pot, then behind the lever. Soul back into the Mace.

Loadout One blunt body (Mace or Hammer) + one nimble bait (Dagger or Sword).

Litmus A/C: no goblin picks up a human and uses him as a club; only something it believes is a dead object is carried into the pen and swung, while its owner steers the blow from another body. B: only a bodiless soul passes the fence and a pot lid to look inside.

Scaling 1: potter only, 1 bell pot. 2: + 1 sleeper in the pen. 3: 2 sleepers, 2 bell pots, a hostile guard outside harasses the bait (third player holds him). 4: same + potter tires of a club after two swings and drops it inside (re-offer at the sill).

Reset Pots and lever latch. A bell means a fight, never a dead end. The club can always launch free; pen gate opens with the lever.

Kit Pot (+ contents), ImpactLever, Door_Lever, sleepers. NEW SoulGrate, GoblinWielder.

10 · Carry Room L-shape · hard

Puzzles A wall with no way over; the only door is the porters' own gate. A Two mats, two porters. The armourer takes metal to the guardroom stand (right). The woodcutter takes wooden things to the firewood bin (a sealed dead end). Each carries one thing at a time and then rests. B Tailgate. The gate is open only while a loaded porter passes. C Guardroom. Two guards play dice by the stand; they look up on the clock.

Team P1 lies still on the metal mat and is carried. P2 follows in the porter's blind spot and slips through the gate behind him, freezing whenever he pauses. In the guardroom both wait as clutter for the guards' heads to drop, then go: a blade sticks into the rafters and leaves along them; a blunt body crosses the floor in the gaps. The last one out hits the latch lever so the gate stays open for bodies left behind. The Staff must be tailgated through, never offered on a mat.

Solo Be carried. For each extra body: go back as a soul, move it to the mat, ride along inside it (or send it empty and meet it at the stand).

Loadout Any; metal is easy, the Staff is the problem child.

Litmus: a human cannot be tidied away as lost property and carried through the enemy's own door, nor sit on a stand between two guards being furniture.

Scaling 1: guards look up rarely. 2: normal. 3: a third guard walks the far corridor. 4: + the armourer refuses to carry while it can see more than one weapon on the mat (offer one at a time, hide the rest).

Reset Firewood bin has a one-way flap back to the mats. Moving in a porter's hands = dropped + a fight, then calm and retry. Latch lever ends the puzzle for good.

11 · Magnet Room long gallery · hard

Puzzles A Crane. A magnet block rides a clock rail over a wide trench; it carries only metal bodies. B Keystones. Two pillars in the trench, reachable only from the crane, each with a socket; while a metal body is seated, plank spans slide out on both sides. C Wooden lock. The exit socket sits in a wall magnet's pull: metal bodies are dragged off it. Only the Staff can seat there (latches).

Team P1 and P2 ride the crane, launch off it onto the pillars and seat. P3 (Staff) crosses the planks at once, because the far landing's goblins have seen the keystones move and come to knock them out: P4 (or P1 after un-seating) holds them off. Staff seats at C, the exit latches, keystones un-seat and crane across.

Solo Crane the Sword to pillar 1, seat; soul to Dagger, pillar 2, seat; soul to Staff, cross, seat C. Then collect. Go quietly: a seated body that nobody saw move is scenery.

Loadout Staff + two metal bodies (three at 3-4 players). No Staff = back to room 9.

Litmus: a magnet does not lift a person, and the bridge exists only while its keystones sit there: keystones that then pull themselves out and fly on. A human team would have to abandon two people on pillars.

Scaling 1-2: two pillars, 0 / 1 goblin. 3: three pillars, 2 goblins. 4: three pillars, 3 goblins, faster crane.

Reset Trench has a ramp back to the start; planks retract cleanly (a rider drops into the trench). C latches.

Kit MagnetZone on a ClockMover, MagnetZone (wall), Door. NEW WeaponSocket (metal-only x2-3, wooden-only x1).

12 · Beam Climb tall shaft · hard, vertical

Puzzles No ledges, only wooden posts and beams, and the heavy bodies needed later must come up too. A Paddle hoist. A platform runs up and down the shaft on the clock without stopping, too fast to step off. B Wooden gear. A blade stuck into the gear jams the hoist exactly where it is; pulling out frees it. C Rung gaps. Between hoist and the high exit: bare wood that only blades hold.

Team P1 (Dagger) climbs the posts to the gear. P3 (Mace) rides the hoist and calls "now" level with the first beam; P1 sticks into the gear; the hoist freezes; P3 steps off. P2 (Sword) sticks in as a rung, P1 frees the gear and leapfrogs up as the next rung. Repeat to the top; the last rung follows.

Solo Hop the Mace onto the hoist at its bottom turn and leave it riding as freight. Soul up to the Dagger waiting at the gear; stick in when the Mace is level with the beam; soul back to the Mace, step off. Then one mind alternates rung, climber, rung. Slow, many swaps; souls fly the shaft freely.

Loadout Dagger (the only body that reaches the gear) + Sword + whatever heavy body the party needs upstairs.

Litmus: the wedge in the machine listens for the call, pulls itself out and then becomes the next rung; rungs never have to be fetched. A human who jams a gear with a dagger has to climb back for it.

Scaling 1: 2 rung gaps, slow hoist. 2: 3 gaps. 3-4: 4 gaps, faster hoist, and a goblin at a window who pokes out any stuck blade he saw move (freeze while his shutter is open; shutters follow the clock).

Reset Falls land on the shaft floor (no damage pit). Un-jamming always restores the clock schedule.

13 · Counterweight Room ring, three levels · very hard

Puzzles Floor, middle ring, top ring. A Pair one (floor to middle): a heavy anchor on one pan raises lighter bodies on the other. B The Hammer cannot jump and nothing out-weighs it: it goes up the goblins' own stair, carried by a porter as junk.
