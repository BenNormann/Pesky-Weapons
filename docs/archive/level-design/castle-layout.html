Castle layout: two towers, a bridge and a keep

You wake as ordinary possessed weapons, locked in the cellars under the back tower. The door at the tower's base is barred from outside, so the only way is up. Both towers are tall, slender and round, one room per floor, and the Arch Mage's castle is bigger on the inside: a doorway with a glowing purple frame is magic. Launch through it and, with a quick flash, you come out of its twin door on another floor with your speed and direction kept. Goblins never use these doors. From the top of the back tower a real suspended bridge (broken in the middle) crosses to the main tower; a few floors up is the Magic Orb, then it is floor after floor down, the Orb rolling where the others cannot, out through the keep to the front doors, where the arch mage's disowned Disciple bursts in. Bring the Orb.

1. Side-on X-ray

One floor slice = one room. Circles are rooms in path order, F is the floor, the small icon is the room's shape. The lift shaft, the orb pipe and the well are real vertical spaces: they are what makes the height felt.

2. Maze map: back tower (which door leads where)

A logical map, not a floor plan: each cell is a room on its own floor (see the X-ray), each orange arrow is a doorway, and from floor to floor that doorway is magic. Dashed purple = side room. Purple bar = locked door. The centre is the lift shaft, switched on from the top (19). Corner icon = room shape.

3. Maze map: main tower (up to the Orb, then all the way down)

Same kind of map. The bridge comes in at 24. The centre is the Orb Room with its orb pipe, and lower down the well: a one-way drop shortcut.

4. All rooms in path order

Green = rest / anvil. Yellow = the Orb. Red = boss. Purple = side rooms (letters). NEW = a regular weapon is found here.

Shapes: Round (12-sided) Octagon Hexagon Wedge Ring (round a shaft) Long gallery Tall shaft L-shape Crescent Cellar (1-5, already built). Walls are straight segments so rebounds stay readable; round rooms get a disc floor.
